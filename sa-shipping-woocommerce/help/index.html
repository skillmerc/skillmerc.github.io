The Courier Guy, Fastway, Pudo and PAXI shipping for WooCommerce: guides

Fixes and setup guides for the South African couriers WooCommerce stores use most.

  • PAXI on WooCommerce: how to let customers pick a PAXI point at checkout
    PAXI has no official WooCommerce integration. Three ways South African stores offer PAXI delivery today, from a manual flat rate to a PAXI point picker on the checkout.
  • Pudo locker shipping in WooCommerce: live rates and a locker picker
    How to offer Pudo (The Courier Guy Locker) delivery in a WooCommerce store: what you need from Pudo, how box sizes are priced, and how customers choose a locker at checkout.
  • The Courier Guy not showing at WooCommerce checkout: causes and fixes
    Customers see "No shipping options" or The Courier Guy rate is missing at checkout. The usual causes (zones, address fields, API keys, missing product weights) and how to check each one.
  • Fastway rates in WooCommerce: how to show live Fastway Couriers prices at checkout
    There is no Fastway South Africa plugin in the WordPress.org directory. Three ways to charge Fastway shipping in WooCommerce, from flat rates per zone to live rates from the free Fastway API.
  • The Courier Guy rates wrong in WooCommerce: why the price is too high or too low
    Your checkout shows a different Courier Guy price than your portal. The usual causes (volumetric weight, declared value, VAT added twice, missing product sizes, caching) and how to check each one.
  • Print a The Courier Guy waybill from WooCommerce: three ways and the common problems
    How to get a The Courier Guy waybill PDF for a WooCommerce order: the portal, an order-screen plugin or the API. Why the waybill is missing, duplicate shipments, expired links and wrong parcel sizes.
  • Pudo locker option not showing at WooCommerce checkout: causes and fixes
    The Pudo locker method is missing at checkout, or customers cannot pick a locker. The usual causes (zone, parcel too big or heavy for a locker box, API key mode, no locker found) and how to check each one.
  • Add The Courier Guy tracking number to WooCommerce order emails
    Three ways to send customers their The Courier Guy tracking reference from WooCommerce: a customer note, a tracking extension or an add-on that books the shipment. Why the number is missing and how to fix it.
  • The Courier Guy WooCommerce plugin is being retired: what to do
    The Courier Guy says it is moving away from its WooCommerce plugin to TCG Connect. What it has said, what happens to your current install, and how to keep live The Courier Guy rates at checkout.
