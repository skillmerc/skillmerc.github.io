WooCommerce free shipping over an amount

2 min readShipping for WooCommerce

Short answer: in WooCommerce, go to Settings, Shipping, open your zone, add the "Free shipping" method and set "Free shipping requires" to "A minimum order amount", then enter the amount. Orders at or over it can pick free shipping.

The catch with the built-in method

WooCommerce's Free shipping is a separate option. Above the amount the customer sees it next to your paid rates, and can still pick a paid one. Below it, only the paid rates show. If you want one courier option that simply drops to R0 above the amount, set the amount on the courier method itself.

With live courier rates

Our free plugin, Skillmerc Shipping for The Courier Guy, Pudo and PAXI, has a "Free shipping over (R)" setting on each method: The Courier Guy, Pudo, Fastway and PAXI.

  1. Go to WooCommerce, Settings, Shipping and open your South Africa zone.
  2. Edit the method, for example "The Courier Guy (door to door)".
  3. Enter the amount in "Free shipping over (R)" and save. Leave it empty to always charge.
  4. When the value of the products in the cart reaches that amount, every rate of that method shows at R0. Below it, the normal rate shows: the live rate with your markup, or your fixed PAXI price.

Each method has its own amount, so Pudo can go free at one amount and The Courier Guy at another. If the courier's rates call fails, the fallback rate shows instead, and it is free above the amount too.

Check it

Add products to the cart just under and just over your amount, enter a South African address, and look at the shipping options at checkout both times.
