The Courier Guy API key: where to find it and how to use it

2 min readShipping for WooCommerce

Short answer: sign in to The Courier Guy portal at portal.thecourierguy.co.za and create a key under Settings, API keys. The Courier Guy says only the account owner sees the Settings menu. For testing, sign up for a free sandbox at sandbox.shiplogic.com and create a sandbox key there. The Courier Guy's rates come from the ShipLogic API, so the sandbox lives on ShipLogic.

Live key or sandbox key

  • A live key works against The Courier Guy's live API and gives your real prices.
  • A sandbox key works against the ShipLogic sandbox and gives test prices.
  • They are not interchangeable. In our testing a sandbox key worked on the sandbox but got "Authentication failed" on The Courier Guy's live API. Whatever plugin you use, its test or sandbox switch must match the key.

Where to paste it in WooCommerce

With our free plugin, Skillmerc Shipping for The Courier Guy, Pudo and PAXI:

  1. Go to WooCommerce, Settings, Shipping and open your South Africa zone.
  2. Add or edit "The Courier Guy (door to door)".
  3. Set "Environment" to Sandbox (test account) or Live, to match the key.
  4. Paste the key into "API key" and save. Fill in your sender address in the same screen.
  5. Add a product to the cart and check the rates at checkout with a South African address.

The key is only sent to The Courier Guy's API when rates are worked out. When a call fails, the plugin writes a line under WooCommerce, Status, Logs (source skillmerc-shipping-courier-guy-pudo-paxi) without your key in it, and shows your fallback rate.

Keep it safe

Treat the key like a password: anyone who has it can use your account through the API. If you think it leaked, delete it in the portal and create a new one.

For the full setup, see The Courier Guy WooCommerce integration, step by step.
