This plugin adds four shipping methods for South African WooCommerce stores:

  • The Courier Guy (door to door): live rates from the ShipLogic API that The Courier Guy uses. Each service level on your account (for example Economy) shows as its own option, or you can limit it to the codes you choose.
  • Pudo locker: door to locker rates from Pudo (The Courier Guy Locker). The customer picks a locker at checkout, and the plugin quotes the smallest locker box (XS to XL) that fits the cart.
  • Fastway (door to door): live rates from Fastway Couriers South Africa. You choose the Fastway franchise that collects your parcels, and the customer sees each Fastway parcel service for their address (for example Local or Road), with satchel prices as an option. It uses a free Fastway API key; the default limit is 100 calls a day and rates are cached to save calls.
  • PAXI (collect at a PEP store): the customer picks a PAXI point on the official PAXI map at checkout. PAXI has no public rates API, so you set the Economy and Speed prices yourself (defaults R59.95 and R109.95; check your current PAXI price list).

The Courier Guy, Pudo and Fastway methods share these settings in each shipping zone (Fastway uses a pickup franchise in place of the sandbox switch and sender address):

  • Sandbox or live API key
  • Sender address
  • Default parcel size and weight for products without dimensions
  • Markup in rand and percent (or a discount with a negative number)
  • Free shipping over an order amount
  • A fallback flat rate that shows when the courier's rates call fails, times out or returns nothing, so a rates outage does not leave customers with "no shipping method". The Pudo method is hidden instead while its locker list cannot be loaded (see the FAQ)

The PAXI method has its own prices, labels, an optional maximum cart weight and a free shipping threshold.

The plugin does not change your billing or shipping address fields.

Locker and PAXI point picker

When the Pudo method is selected, a locker list appears under the shipping options. Lockers in the customer's postal code, town and province are listed first, and a search box filters the list. The chosen locker is checked against the Pudo locker list.

When a PAXI rate is selected, a "Choose a PAXI point" button opens the PAXI map. The point the customer picks is checked (it must be a PAXI node code such as P1234) and the order cannot be placed without one.

The chosen locker or PAXI point is saved on the order and shown on the admin order screen, the order received page and order emails.

Checkout block and classic checkout

Both pickers work on the Checkout block (the default on new WooCommerce stores) and on the classic [woocommerce_checkout] shortcode checkout. On the Checkout block the picker appears below the shipping options and shows up to 200 lockers, best address match first. The choice is saved through the WooCommerce Store API and checked again on the server when the order is placed.

What this version does not do

Version 0.1 quotes rates and records the locker or PAXI point. It does not book collections, print waybills or send tracking updates. Book shipments in your The Courier Guy, Pudo, Fastway or PAXI account as usual. The optional Pro version adds booking for The Courier Guy.

External services

This plugin connects to these services. It sends data only when rates are calculated in the cart or checkout, when the Pudo locker list is refreshed (at most every 12 hours), or when the customer opens the PAXI map.

  • ShipLogic / The Courier Guy (api.shiplogic.com for sandbox keys, api.portal.thecourierguy.co.za for live keys): used for door to door rates. Sends your sender address, the customer's delivery address (street, suburb, city, province, postal code, company), the parcel size and weight, and the cart value as declared value. Terms: thecourierguy.co.za/terms-conditions Privacy: thecourierguy.co.za/privacy-policy
  • Pudo (The Courier Guy Locker): used for locker rates and the locker list. Rates (sandbox.api-pudo.co.za for sandbox, api-tcg.co.za for live): sends your API key, your sender address, the chosen locker code and the parcel size and weight. Locker list (Pudo's public list: sandbox.api-pudo.co.za/api/v1/guest/lockers-data for sandbox, api-pudo.co.za/api/v1/guest/lockers-data for live): a plain download that sends no API key and nothing about your store's orders or customers. Like every request WordPress makes, it comes from your server's IP address and carries your site address in the user agent. If the public list cannot be loaded, the plugin asks the rates host for the list with your API key, as versions before 0.1.4 did. Pudo is now The Courier Guy Locker, covered by the same terms (thecourierguy.co.za/terms-conditions) and privacy policy (thecourierguy.co.za/privacy-policy).
  • Fastway Couriers South Africa (sa.api.fastway.org): used for Fastway door to door rates. Sends your API key, your pickup franchise code, the customer's town or suburb and postal code, and the parcel weight. Website terms: fastway.co.za/terms-and-conditions/website-terms-of-use Privacy: fastway.co.za/media/2467/privacy-and-protection-of-personal-information-fastway-june-2021.pdf
  • PAXI map (map.paxi.co.za, run by PAXI, part of the Pepkor group): when the customer clicks "Choose a PAXI point", the map loads in a frame in the customer's browser. The plugin sends nothing to PAXI itself; the map page is loaded from PAXI's servers (which see the customer's IP address and browser details) and it may use Google Maps and Google Analytics. Only the chosen point (code, name, address) comes back to your store. Terms and privacy policy: paxi.co.za/terms-conditions

You need your own account and API key with The Courier Guy and Pudo, and a free Fastway API key from sa.api.fastway.org for Fastway rates. PAXI parcels are sent through your own PAXI account or a PEP store.

Installation

  1. Install and activate the plugin. WooCommerce must be active.
  2. Go to WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping, open your South Africa zone and add "The Courier Guy (door to door)", "Pudo locker", "Fastway (door to door)" and/or "PAXI (collect at a PEP store)".
  3. Edit each method. For The Courier Guy and Pudo: choose Sandbox or Live, paste the API key, fill in your sender address, and check the default parcel and fallback rate. For Fastway: paste your free Fastway API key, pick the franchise that collects your parcels, and check the default parcel and fallback rate. For PAXI: set your prices. Sandbox accounts are free: sandbox.shiplogic.com for The Courier Guy and sandbox.pudo.co.za for Pudo. Sandbox prices are test prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support the Checkout block?

Yes. The Courier Guy rates, the Pudo locker picker and the PAXI point picker work on the Checkout block and on the classic checkout.

Can a customer order PAXI or Pudo without choosing a point?

No. The order is blocked with a message until a locker or PAXI point is chosen.

What happens if the courier API is down?

The Courier Guy and Fastway methods show your fallback rate instead of disappearing. Pudo does the same when its rates call fails. When Pudo's locker list cannot be loaded, the Pudo method is hidden instead, because a customer cannot choose a locker without the list. The locker list comes from Pudo's public list, which needs no API key; if that fails, the plugin asks for the list with your API key. The plugin keeps the last good locker list for up to 7 days and uses it while Pudo's list is down. Failed calls are logged under WooCommerce > Status > Logs (source skillmerc-shipping-courier-guy-pudo-paxi) without your API key, and the plugin waits two minutes before trying the API again so checkout stays fast. PAXI uses your fixed prices and does not call an API for rates.

Pudo is missing at checkout or the locker list does not load. What can I check?

From version 0.1.4 the locker list is downloaded from Pudo's public list and does not depend on your API key. If Pudo still does not show: check that an API key is saved on the Pudo locker method (rates need it), and look under WooCommerce > Status > Logs (source skillmerc-shipping-courier-guy-pudo-paxi) for a line that names lockers-data. A line with an HTTP status code is an answer from Pudo's server. A line with a cURL error means your web server could not reach api-pudo.co.za; ask your host to allow outgoing HTTPS requests to that address. The plugin tries again every two minutes, and Pudo returns by itself once the list loads. Pudo is also hidden when the cart does not fit the largest locker box.

How is the parcel size worked out?

Product weights are added up. The parcel uses the largest length, width and height across the products in the cart. Products without a weight or dimensions use the defaults from the method settings. For Pudo, the smallest locker box that fits the parcel size, volume and weight is quoted. If nothing fits the largest box, the Pudo method is not shown.

Are rates cached?

Yes, each quote is cached for 10 minutes per address and parcel, and the locker list for 12 hours.

How do I get a Fastway API key?

Go to sa.api.fastway.org, open Get API Key, choose South Africa and register. The key arrives on screen and by email. Paste it into the Fastway method in your shipping zone and pick the franchise that collects your parcels.

Does this replace The Courier Guy's own plugin?

For live rates at checkout, yes. It quotes The Courier Guy door to door rates (and Pudo locker rates) with your own The Courier Guy API key, on the Checkout block and the classic checkout. The free plugin does not book collections, print waybills or send tracking numbers, which The Courier Guy's plugin can do; book in your The Courier Guy portal, or use the optional Pro add-on for The Courier Guy booking. In November 2026 The Courier Guy told us it is moving away from its older plugins towards its TCG Connect platform, with no shutdown date given. What we know: skillmerc.com/sa-shipping-woocommerce/help/courier-guy-woocommerce-plugin-retired

Is this plugin made by The Courier Guy, Pudo, Fastway or PAXI?

No. It is an independent plugin by Skillmerc that uses their public APIs and map. The names are trademarks of their owners.
