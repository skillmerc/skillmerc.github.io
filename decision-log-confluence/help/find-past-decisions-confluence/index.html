How to find past decisions in Confluence

3 min readDecision Keeper for Confluence

Someone asks why the team chose that vendor, or that database. The answer is somewhere in Confluence, on a page from months ago. Here is how to find it with Confluence alone, and with an app that keeps every decision in one register.

1. With what Confluence already has

We read Atlassian's help pages on 26 November 2026.

  • Search. Confluence search finds words on pages. It works if you know a word from the page title or text. It does not know which pages hold a decision, or which decision still stands.
  • The Decision report macro. Put Decision report on a page. It lists pages with decisions, from the spaces you pick, and you can filter by label or any CQL field. It finds decisions written with Confluence's own Decision element (type /decision). Atlassian's macro page.
  • Labels. If your team labels decision pages, for example decision, you can search by label or feed the label to the Decision report.

All of this depends on people writing decisions the same way every time. Search cannot tell you the owner, the options that were turned down, or whether a later decision replaced this one.

2. With Decision Keeper for Confluence

Decision Keeper keeps each decision as a record with fixed fields: title, status, owner, context, options, rationale, date and history. The Decision Register lists them all, from every space, but only those on pages you are allowed to open. Records are added with the Decision Record macro on a page. How to set it up.

Search the register

  1. Open Apps in the Confluence sidebar and choose Decision Register.
  2. Type in Search. It looks in the title, context, options, rationale, Jira issue key, owner, stakeholders, page title and space key. So "vendor", a person's name or "FIN-42" all work.
  3. Narrow it down with the filters: Status, Format, Owner, Space, Decided from and Decided to. The dates use the day a decision was decided. A record with no decided date uses the day it was created.
  4. Sort by any column: Decision, Status, Owner, Space, Decided or Updated. The newest updates come first.

To see only what still stands, set Status to Decided. Superseded decisions stay in the log, so you can still read why the old choice was made.

Read the full record

Choose Details on a row. You see:

  • whether the decision still stands, or which decision replaced it
  • the context
  • the options considered, with the chosen one marked
  • the decision and the rationale
  • related decisions
  • the history: every status change, with the person and the date
  • a link to the page the decision lives on

Take the list with you

Choose Export CSV to download the rows you have filtered, for an audit or a retrospective.

Check a new page against past decisions

Before a new proposal goes out, check that it does not undo something already decided:

  • On any page: choose Decision check under the page title. It lists the decisions filed on that page and checks the page text against the rest of the log.
  • In the register: choose Check a draft, paste the text and choose Check.

Findings come in four groups: Possible conflict, Relies on a superseded decision, Still being decided and Already decided. Each one links to the record. The check matches words against recorded decisions, so treat a finding as a lead to read, not a verdict. No overlap found does not prove there is no conflict.

Ask in Rovo

If Rovo is on for your site, choose Ask Decision Keeper in the register, or pick the Decision Keeper agent in Rovo chat. Ask in plain words, such as "What have we decided about vendor payment terms?". It answers only from recorded decisions, shows only decisions on pages you can open, and links to the source page.
