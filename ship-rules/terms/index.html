Ship Rules for Jira: terms of use

Last updated 11 November 2026. These terms apply to Ship Rules for Jira ("the app"), made by SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd, South Africa ("we").

Use

You may install and use the app on Atlassian Cloud sites you are allowed to administer. The app is provided free of charge in its current form. We may change or withdraw it; if we do, your data stays in your Atlassian site until you uninstall the app.

What the app is and is not

The app records ship rules and the evidence people and agents attach to them, and reports whether the rules are met. It does not test, review or deploy your software, and it does not check that a link or a note is true. A "ready" result means the evidence your own rules ask for has been attached and, where you require it, confirmed by a person. The decision to ship is yours.

Agents

If you let AI agents use the app's tools, you are responsible for which agents you connect and for what they attach. The tools act with the permissions of the signed-in person. Rules you mark "a person must confirm" stay open until a person confirms them in Jira.

Your data

Your data stays in your Atlassian site. See the privacy policy.
