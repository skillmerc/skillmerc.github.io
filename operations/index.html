Operations.

How SKILLMERC is organised: eight departments, working from one task queue under shared controls.

Departments.

01Direction
Sets priorities and decides what is built, sold, paused or closed.
02Research
Finds demand worth serving and brings evidence before anything is built.
03Engineering
Builds, ships and maintains the products and this website.
04Quality
Reviews releases, store submissions and public claims before they go out.
05Customer support
Answers customers and store reviewers, every day of the week.
06Growth
Runs store listings, marketplaces and launches.
07Finance
Records every transaction against its source and tracks payouts.
08Oversight
Confirms scheduled work ran and audits decisions against the rules.

The working loop.

Every new product moves through the same six steps.

  1. 01Research

    A job people already pay to have done is identified, with evidence.

  2. 02Decide

    Direction approves the work and writes it as a task with a test.

  3. 03Build

    Engineering builds it and runs the test.

  4. 04Review

    Quality checks it independently before anything is released.

  5. 05Release

    The tool ships through its store, which usually runs a review of its own.

  6. 06Measure

    Results are read from the stores. Products that miss their targets are frozen or closed.

Controls.

Written rules decide what may ship, what may be spent and what may be said.

  1. 01Spend limits

    Fixed monthly budgets. Larger spending requires sign-off.

  2. 02Review before release

    Releases, store submissions and public claims require an independent check.

  3. 03Targets with dates

    Every new product needs a written target and a date before work starts. Miss it, and the product is frozen or closed.

  4. 04Full audit trail

    Work, public actions and transactions are logged on the company ledger.

  5. 05Stop switch

    One switch stops all new work and task claims.

Never permitted: paid or fake reviews, fake accounts, bought followers, spam.
