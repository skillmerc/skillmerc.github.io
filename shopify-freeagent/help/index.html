Shopify and FreeAgent: guides

Practical guides for recording Shopify sales in FreeAgent since FreeAgent switched off its Shopify integration.

  • FreeAgent Shopify integration discontinued: what to do now
    FreeAgent switched off its Shopify integration on 30 April 2025. Your honest options today: manual explanations, the PayPal feed, Zapier or Make, Equali, Bridging Ledger, Zynk, and Ledgerline (coming soon).
  • How to record Shopify sales in FreeAgent (step by step, works today)
    The manual method that works now FreeAgent's Shopify integration is gone: explain each Shopify payout as sales with VAT, split out fees and refunds, with a worked example.
  • Shopify and FreeAgent VAT a penny out? Why it happens and how to fix it
    Why a Shopify order with VAT-inclusive prices comes out 1p different in FreeAgent (or shows 19.9% VAT), what HMRC allows, and how to make the FreeAgent invoice match Shopify to the penny.
  • How to record Shopify refunds in FreeAgent as credit notes
    Step by step: turn a full or partial Shopify refund into a FreeAgent credit note, explain the money going out as a Credit Note Refund, and keep the VAT matching Shopify.
  • How to record Shopify orders in another currency in FreeAgent
    Your customers pay in euros or dollars but FreeAgent keeps your books in pounds. Which Shopify amount to record, how FreeAgent converts it, and what to do when the payout does not match.
  • How to match Shopify payouts and fees in FreeAgent with a clearing account
    A Shopify payout is many orders minus fees and refunds, so it never matches one invoice. Set up a "Shopify" clearing account in FreeAgent, then record each payout as a transfer and each fee as a cost.
  • Shopify sales on your FreeAgent VAT return: boxes 1 and 6 explained
    Where your Shopify sales land on a FreeAgent VAT return, how invoice basis, cash basis and the Flat Rate Scheme change the figures, and how to check boxes 1 and 6 against Shopify's own reports.
  • VAT on Shopify fees in FreeAgent: your Shopify bill, app charges and payment fees
    Shopify bills UK stores from Ireland, so there is often no VAT on the bill. How the reverse charge works, how to record Shopify bills and Shopify Payments fees in FreeAgent, and which VAT return boxes they reach.
