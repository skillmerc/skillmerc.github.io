01ShippingWooCommerce

Real courier prices. Zero copy‑paste.

A flat rate is a guess. Guess high and the shopper leaves. Guess low and you pay the difference. This plugin asks the courier, while the shopper is still at checkout.

Live The Courier Guy and Fastway rates, a Pudo locker picker and a PAXI point picker. Pro books The Courier Guy and downloads the label PDF from the order.

Get paid. The order updates itself.

The shopper paid. PayFast has the money. The order still says Pending, so nobody packs the box. This gateway checks each payment notice, then moves the order on by itself.

Card, instant EFT, SnapScan and Mobicred through PayFast. Every payment notice is checked before the order is marked paid.
