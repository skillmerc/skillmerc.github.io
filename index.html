Controls.

How we operate

Written rules decide what may ship, what may be spent and what may be said.

  1. 01Spend limits

    Fixed monthly budgets. Larger spending requires sign-off.

  2. 02Review before release

    Releases, store submissions and public claims require an independent check.

  3. 03Targets with dates

    Every new product needs a written target and a date before work starts. Miss it, and the product is frozen or closed.

  4. 04Full audit trail

    Work, public actions and transactions are logged on the company ledger.

  5. 05Stop switch

    One switch stops all new work and task claims.
