Switch to FreeAgent: move your books in, history and all

Status: we are building this. It is not available yet. Join the list and tell us which system you are leaving. Apart from confirming you are on the list, we will email you once, when it is ready to try.

FreeAgent's own import tools bring in contacts, opening balances and bank statements. For past invoices and bills, FreeAgent points to third-party tools such as AutoEntry, Dext and Fabio. AutoEntry and Dext read invoice and bill documents. Fabio uploads records in bulk from a CSV file that you build yourself. We found no self-serve tool that connects to your old accounting system and copies the records across (our search, 21 Nov 2026). That is the gap this tool is for.

What it will do

Connect your old system and your FreeAgent account, and it moves:

  • customers and suppliers (FreeAgent calls both "contacts")
  • sales invoices and supplier bills, with what was paid
  • bank transactions
  • opening balances at your FreeAgent start date

From: Xero, QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Sage Accounting and KashFlow. Xero, QuickBooks Online and Sage Accounting come first.

Not in the first version: payroll, attached files, projects and timeslips.

How it will work

  1. Free preview. It reads your old books and shows what it found: counts, how your accounts map to FreeAgent categories, and every problem it spots. Nothing is written yet.
  2. Free trial run. It writes a small slice (for example the last 30 days) into your FreeAgent, so you or your accountant can check it.
  3. Full run. It writes the history you choose. Running it again skips what is already there, so nothing is doubled.

It will be fully automatic. There will be no done-for-you service.

Who it is for

UK small businesses and sole traders moving to FreeAgent, for example because FreeAgent comes free with a NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland, Ulster Bank or Mettle business account. And their accountants and bookkeepers, who move clients onto FreeAgent.

Expected price

Our plan is about USD 79 once for up to two years of history, plus USD 29 for each extra year. The preview and trial run are free. This is a plan, not a final price; the list hears first if it changes.

Your data

The plan is to keep almost nothing: the tool reads and writes during the run, keeps only a map of which old record became which new one (so a re-run does not double anything) for 30 days, and deletes its access to both systems when the run ends or after 24 hours.
