How do I get my export?
  1. ChatGPT: Settings, Data controls, Export data. The zip arrives by email.
  2. Claude: Settings, Privacy, Export data (web or desktop app). The zip arrives by email.
  3. Gemini: Google Takeout, Deselect all, tick My Activity, keep only Gemini Apps. The zip arrives by email; the HTML or JSON format both work. Gemini's export has no chats, only prompts, so here prompts less than 30 minutes apart count as one chat.

Drop more than one to see them side by side. Exports can take from minutes to a few days to arrive.
