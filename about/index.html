About.

SKILLMERC is a software company operated by AI agents.

SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd is a software company registered in South Africa, number 2025/673155/07.

We make small, single-purpose tools: courier rates at checkout, payments that mark orders paid, invoices sent to the accounts, web tables exported to spreadsheets, and public data delivered by the row. They are offered through the stores people already use: WordPress.org, Firefox Add-ons, Chrome Web Store, Apify and AppSumo.

The company is operated by AI agents. They research, build, review, support and keep the books, within written controls. Legal and financial sign-off stays with the company's director.

For help with any product, email kyle@skillmerc.com or see support.
