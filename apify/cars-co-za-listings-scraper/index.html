Open in Apify StoreExample: Toyota Hilux prices and mileage in Gauteng

Get new and used car listings from Cars.co.za, South Africa's large car marketplace, as clean JSON or CSV. Each listing comes with price in Rand, year, mileage, make, model, variant, body type, transmission, fuel type, colour, province, city, dealer name and the listing link. Filter by make, model, province and maximum price.

Prices, mileage and years are read from the structured data the site itself uses, then checked for sense (price between R1,000 and R50 million, mileage up to 1.5 million km). A value that fails the check is returned as empty rather than wrong.

Input example

{
  "make": "Toyota",
  "model": "Hilux",
  "province": "Gauteng",
  "maxPriceZAR": 500000,
  "maxResults": 100
}
Field What it does
make Car make, for example Toyota, BMW, Volkswagen. Leave blank for all makes.
model Car model, for example Hilux, Polo, 3 Series. Needs a make.
province One of the nine provinces. Leave blank for the whole country.
maxPriceZAR Highest price in Rand. Leave blank for no limit.
maxResults Stop after this many listings (up to 10,000). Each results page holds 20.

previous_price_zar is filled when the seller has dropped the price. listing_date is not shown in search results and is always empty.

Pricing

You pay per listing returned, plus a tiny fee when the run starts. Platform usage is included.

Common uses

  • Track used car prices for a make and model over time
  • Build valuation or price-comparison data for the South African market
  • Watch dealer stock and price drops
  • Feed listings into a spreadsheet, CRM or alert flow

Limits

  • Only what the public search results show. Seller phone numbers and full descriptions are not included.
  • The search covers both new and used listings, as on the site.
  • If the site changes its layout, the scraper falls back to reading the listing cards and keeps the same sanity checks.
