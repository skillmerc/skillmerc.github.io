How to get a back in stock alert for a product page in Firefox

2 min readChangekeep

The shop has no "email me" button, or it never sends the email. You can watch the stock label yourself and get a notification when it changes.

With Changekeep

  1. Install Changekeep from Firefox Add-ons. It needs Firefox 142 or later, on a computer.
  2. Open the product page. Click the Changekeep button, point at the stock label, such as "Out of stock", and click it.
  3. Check the value in the panel and click "Save watch". The first time you watch a site, allow Changekeep to check it.
  4. When the label changes, you get a notification and a count on the button. The change view shows "Was" and "Now".

You can watch the price on the same page the same way, to see when it drops.

Free

10 watches, each checked as often as every 30 minutes. Pro is coming soon and is not on sale yet.

Limits

  • Checks run only while Firefox is open. After the computer sleeps, each watch that is due is checked once.
  • If the shop draws the label with scripts after the page loads, a background check cannot see it. Changekeep tells you when you pick it.
  • Check that the shop's terms allow automatic checks.
