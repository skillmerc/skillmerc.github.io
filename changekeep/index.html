Changekeep: get an alert when part of a web page changes

Changekeep is a browser add-on by SKILLMERC. You click the part of a page you care about, such as a price, a stock label or a list, and Changekeep tells you when that part changes.

Add to Firefox, free

Free and Pro

What people watch

  • A price. Pick the price on a shop page and get an alert when it changes. Pro, when it comes, will also let you alert only when it drops below a value you set.
  • A stock label. Watch "Sold out" and hear when it goes away.
  • A list. A job board, a notice page or a news list: get an alert when a new line appears.
  • A status line. An appointment, exam or ticket page that says "no dates available": get an alert when the words change.

How it works

  1. Open the page and press the Changekeep button in the toolbar. Point at the part you want and click it, or choose "Watch the whole page".
  2. Choose what to compare (the text, the number in it, or whether it is there at all) and how often to check, then save the watch. The watch list then asks you to allow Changekeep to open that one site. The first check records the starting value and never alerts.
  3. When the part changes you get a browser notification and a count on the toolbar button. The change view shows the text before and after, word by word, and one line that says why it alerted.

What keeps it calm

Page change alerts are only useful if they are real. Changekeep compares only the part you picked, after cleaning it. These cleaning rules are on by default: extra spaces, invisible characters, scripts and styles, hidden parts, ad containers, times like "5 minutes ago", and tracking codes in links.

  • A part that is missing, or a page that did not load, shows as a problem on your watch list. It is never reported as a change.
  • If an alert was noise, press "Not a real change? Ignore changes like this" on the change view. Changekeep tunes that watch, for example to ignore clock times like 10:42 or changes of only a few words.
  • When Pro comes, its rule alerts will fire once, when the number crosses your value. A price that stays under it will not alert again.
  • Quiet hours, or a mode with no notifications at all, only the count on the button.

Honest limits

  • Checks run only while your browser is open. After the computer sleeps, each watch that is due is checked once.
  • Some pages draw their content with scripts after they load. Changekeep 1.0 cannot watch those parts and says so when you pick one. Try a larger part or the whole page.
  • Changekeep does not follow a page that now sends visitors to another address. It tells you, and you can move the watch to the new address and keep its history.
  • Pages behind a sign-in work only if you tick "This page needs me signed in" for that watch. If you get signed out, the watch shows a problem instead of a false change.
  • It asks each site at most once a minute, whatever the number of watches. Check that the site's terms allow automatic checks.
  • Alerts are browser notifications. There is no email or text message alert, no sync between computers and no screenshot comparison.
  • Needs Firefox 142 or later.

Privacy

Changekeep contacts only the sites you choose to watch and, for Pro, our licence provider Freemius. Nothing is sent to SKILLMERC: no account, no analytics.

To check a watch, the add-on loads that page from your browser, on the schedule you set. The site sees an ordinary visit. Your watches, the text it reads and the changes stay in your browser. Full details: Changekeep privacy policy.
