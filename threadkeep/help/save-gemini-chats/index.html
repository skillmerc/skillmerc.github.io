How to save Gemini chats

5 min readThreadkeep

Gemini can send one response to Google Docs, and Google Takeout can download all your Gemini activity. To save a whole chat as one file you have open, use your browser's print window or a browser add-on.

We read Google's Gemini Apps help pages on 24 November 2026. Here is what each way gives you.

1. Export one response to Google Docs

By Google's page on exporting responses, on gemini.google.com:

  1. Below the response, click Share & export. If you do not see it, look in the More (three dots) menu below the response.
  2. Click Export to Docs. This saves a new doc in Google Drive.

The same menu has Draft in Gmail, and a table has its own Export to Sheets button at its bottom right. Google says export options vary by Gemini app and, for a Google Workspace account, by its settings.

To turn the doc into a file, open it in Google Docs and click File > Download, then pick PDF or Microsoft Word (.docx) (Google Docs help).

A Deep Research report has its own Share & export button in the panel on the right, with Export to Docs (Google's Deep Research page).

Google's page describes this per response, not for the whole chat. Some guides mention a whole-chat option in the chat's own menu. We could not find it on Google's help pages, so check your menu.

2. Google Takeout: all your Gemini activity

By Google's page on downloading your Gemini Apps data:

  1. Go to takeout.google.com, signed in to the same Google Account you use for Gemini.
  2. Click Deselect all.
  3. Tick My Activity, click All activity data included, click Deselect all in the pop-up, tick Gemini Apps and click Ok. (The box named "Gemini" on the main list is your Gems data, not your chats.)
  4. Click Next step, choose how to get the archive and click Create export.

Google says it can take a few hours to a few days, and that most people get the link the same day. An emailed download link is active for 7 days. This is a backup of your activity, not a neat file of one chat.

3. A public link

Below a response, click Share, then Share conversation. This makes a public g.co/gemini/share link to the whole conversation. By Google's sharing page, anyone with the link can read the chat and reshare it, and in most cases continue it with Gemini on their own. The page shows the chat as it was when you made the link. It is a web address, not a file.

4. Print the page to PDF

Open the chat on a computer, press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on a Mac) and choose "Save to PDF" (Firefox) or "Save as PDF" (Chrome). This needs nothing extra.

The print window prints the page as it is, with the side bar and buttons around the chat. On a long chat, scroll to the top first, then check that the PDF has every message before you rely on it.

5. Threadkeep, an add-on for Firefox and Chrome

Threadkeep is a free add-on we make. Version 2.0 is on Firefox; the Chrome update is in review. Gemini works in both versions on the stores today: Firefox 2.0.0 and Chrome 1.2.0. It saves the Gemini chat you have open as one file.

  1. Install Threadkeep from Firefox Add-ons or the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the chat on gemini.google.com and click the purple Export button at the bottom right of the page.
  3. The first time in version 2.0, a short notice says what is read. Nothing is read until you click I agree.
  4. Click Markdown, Text, JSON, Copy, PDF or Word. In Firefox 2.0.0 you can also click Save to library.
  5. On a long chat Threadkeep loads the earlier messages once, after your click. Do not scroll while it reads. The panel then says how many of your messages and how many replies it saved.

What you get:

  • PDF opens the chat in a new tab with the browser's print window. Choose "Save to PDF" or "Save as PDF". The text is real text you can select and search.
  • Word downloads a .doc file that Word opens (an HTML .doc file, not a .docx).
  • Markdown, Text and JSON download a file. Copy puts the chat on the clipboard as Markdown.
  • Code blocks keep their indentation, tables stay tables, and lists and links stay.

Threadkeep also works in Gemini Notebook (notebook.google.com, formerly NotebookLM). There it reads only the chat, not your sources or notes.

Limits

  • Free: Markdown, text, JSON and copy have no limit. PDF and Word are 3 exports a day. Pro removes that limit and costs USD 4.99 a month, USD 29 a year or USD 59 once, for one browser. You get it from Pro and license in the panel.
  • Not saved: pictures (a picture stays as a short line, or as a link in Markdown) and uploaded files. A reply that is only a picture, a file or a canvas is kept as a short line, without its content.
  • Chrome 1.2.0 has no library, and on Gemini it does not support maths or the reasoning ("thinking") section.
  • It saves the chat you have open, one at a time. For all your activity at once, use Google Takeout.

Questions

Is the chat uploaded anywhere? No. The chat is read in your browser and saved to your computer, or to the library in your browser. Threadkeep needs no account and has no analytics and no ads. The only thing it ever sends is a Pro license key check to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider, and only if you buy Pro. No chat text is in it.

Does it work in the Gemini phone app? No. Threadkeep is a browser add-on. It works on the Gemini website, gemini.google.com, in Firefox or Chrome.
