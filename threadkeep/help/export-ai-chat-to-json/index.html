How to export an AI chat to JSON

2 min readThreadkeep

You want a conversation as data: who said what, in order, in a file a script can read. Threadkeep saves the chat you have open as JSON.

With Threadkeep

  1. Install Threadkeep and open the chat. It works with DeepSeek, Gemini and Gemini Notebook from install. On ChatGPT and Claude, click the Threadkeep button in the toolbar on that tab, or allow the site in its settings.
  2. Open the Threadkeep panel. The first time, a short notice says what is read. Nothing is read until you click "I agree".
  3. Click "JSON".

What the file holds

  • "title", "source" (the chat's address) and "exported_at".
  • "messages": one entry per message, with "role" and "content" (the message as Markdown).
  • On DeepSeek, a "reasoning" field when the reasoning box is ticked.

JSON exports have no limit on the Free plan and never carry the "Saved with Threadkeep" line.

Limits

  • Pictures and uploaded files are not saved. The content of Claude artifacts is not saved; a title line is kept.
  • The chat is read in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Threadkeep is made by SKILLMERC. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or DeepSeek.
