DeepSeek "Length limit reached": how to continue a long chat in a new chat

4 min readThreadkeep

When a DeepSeek chat hits its length limit, DeepSeek points you to a new chat. To keep working, carry what matters from the old chat into the new one: a short summary, or the text of the chat itself.

What DeepSeek says

On 20 November 2026 the text of the web app at chat.deepseek.com held these messages. We read them in the app's text and did not see them on screen in a chat:

  • "Length limit exceeded. Your message will be sent to a new chat."
  • "Length limit reached. Try shortening or start a new chat."
  • "Message limit reached. Please start a new chat."

Older reports quote "Length limit reached. Please start a new chat." We could not confirm on DeepSeek's own pages how long a chat may get, so this page gives no number.

Ways to carry on without an add-on

Ask for a summary before the limit. If the old chat still answers, ask DeepSeek for a short handover: the goal, the decisions made, the current version of the text or code, and the open questions. Paste that into the new chat. This stops working once the old chat takes no more messages, and a summary drops detail.

Copy by hand. Select what you need in the old chat, copy it and paste it into the new chat. DeepSeek's input box has its own limit: the app has the messages "The content is too long to paste into the input field." and "The message you sent is too long. Please modify it and resend." So a long chat has to go across in pieces. And DeepSeek keeps only the messages near the screen in the page, so one selection can miss part of a long chat.

With Threadkeep in Firefox

Threadkeep is an add-on we make for Firefox. It reads the DeepSeek chat you have open and saves it to a file.

The free plan already gives you the whole old chat as one text: click Text, Markdown or Copy. You then cut it into pieces yourself.

Pro adds "Continue in a new chat", which makes the pieces for you:

  1. Install Threadkeep from Firefox Add-ons and open the old chat on chat.deepseek.com.
  2. Click the purple Export button at the bottom right of the page.
  3. Click Continue in a new chat. It is marked PRO. On the free plan the panel says it is a Pro feature: click Pro and license, then Get Pro. After you pay, paste the license key from your purchase email and click Activate. Firefox asks for your permission before the key is checked.
  4. Threadkeep reads the chat and downloads a text file. Its name ends in "-continue.txt".
  5. Open the file. Each part starts under a line that reads "COPY PART 1 OF 3 BELOW THIS LINE", with the real numbers. Paste part 1 into a new DeepSeek chat and send it. When there is more than one part, the note in it asks DeepSeek to reply only "Part received".
  6. Paste the other parts in order. The last part asks DeepSeek for a five line summary of where you were, and then to continue from there.

Each part stays under 24,000 characters and is cut between messages where possible. The reasoning ("thinking") sections are left out to save space.

Limits

  • "Continue in a new chat" is a Pro feature. Pro costs USD 4.99 a month, USD 29 a year or USD 59 once, for one browser.
  • The parts are the full text of the old chat, not a summary. A long chat makes many parts and you paste each one by hand. We have not tested on DeepSeek whether every part fits its input box, or how much of the new chat's room the parts use.
  • Threadkeep cannot change DeepSeek's limit.
  • The old chat must still open on chat.deepseek.com, in Firefox or Chrome. Which sites each version supports.
  • To keep a copy of the old chat too: Markdown, text and JSON are free with no limit. PDF and Word are 3 exports a day on the free plan. The Word file is a .doc, not a .docx.
  • Threadkeep is made by SKILLMERC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or produced by DeepSeek.

Questions

Will waiting reopen the old chat? DeepSeek's messages do not say so. They point to a new chat.

Can this be done without paying? Yes. Export the old chat with Text or Markdown, which has no daily limit, and split the file yourself.

Is the old chat sent anywhere? No. Threadkeep reads it in your browser and saves the file to your computer. Nothing from your chats is ever sent.
