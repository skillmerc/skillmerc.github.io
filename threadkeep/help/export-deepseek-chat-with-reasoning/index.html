How to export a DeepSeek chat with the reasoning included or left out

2 min readThreadkeep

DeepSeek shows its reasoning above each answer. Sometimes you want it in your notes, sometimes only the answer. Here is how to choose.

With Threadkeep

  1. Install Threadkeep and open the DeepSeek chat at chat.deepseek.com.
  2. Click the Export button on the page, or the Threadkeep button in the toolbar. The first time, a short notice says what is read. Nothing is read until you click "I agree".
  3. Tick or untick "Include the reasoning (thinking) section".
  4. Pick a format: Markdown, Text, JSON or Copy, or PDF or Word.

In Markdown, maths keeps its LaTeX, code blocks keep their indentation and tables stay tables. In JSON, the reasoning sits in its own "reasoning" field on each reply, and is left out when you untick the box.

Free and Pro

  • Markdown, text, JSON and Copy have no limit. Free gives 3 PDF or Word exports a day.
  • On the Free plan, exported files (not JSON) end with a short "Saved with Threadkeep" line and a link. You can turn it off in settings. Pro files never have it.

Limits

  • Pictures and uploaded files are not saved. A picture stays as a short line, or as a link in Markdown.
  • The chat is read in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Threadkeep is made by SKILLMERC. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with DeepSeek.
