Threadkeep is a browser extension by SKILLMERC. It saves the AI conversation you have open in your tab to a library in your browser or to a file on your own computer.

What it does

  1. Open a conversation and click the Export button on the page, or the toolbar button. The first time, a short notice says what is read. Nothing is read until you click "I agree".
  2. Press "Save to library" to keep a copy. Save the same conversation again later and the copy is updated.
  3. Or pick a format and save it to your computer straight away: Markdown, plain text, JSON, PDF, Word, or copy to the clipboard.

The library (version 2.0):

  • Search the text of everything you saved. Results show a short snippet, and you can filter by site.
  • Folders, pins and tags. Sort conversations into folders, with one level of subfolders. Pin the ones you use most. Add tags and filter by tag.
  • Open the original conversation on its site with one click.
  • Back up and restore. Back up the whole library to one file and restore it later. A restore adds the chats that are new, replaces a chat you already have with its copy in the file, and keeps every other saved chat.

The export:

  • Real text in PDFs. PDF goes through your browser's own "Save as PDF", so the text can be selected and searched. Word too (an HTML .doc file that Word opens, not a .docx).
  • Code, tables and links. Code blocks keep their indentation, tables stay tables, lists and links stay. On DeepSeek, maths keeps its LaTeX and the reasoning section can be included or left out.
  • Both sides. The panel says how many of your messages and replies it saved. If a site has changed its page and something may be missing, it tells you. On very long conversations, check that count.

What stays on your device: everything

  • Conversations are read in your browser. Saved ones stay in your browser and files go to your computer. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. We run no server that could receive them.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • It reads only the conversation you have open, when you open its panel, export or save. It does not use your login to fetch anything in the background.
  • Firefox 2.0.0 works on chat.deepseek.com, gemini.google.com and notebook.google.com, and on chatgpt.com and claude.ai only after you allow them (see below). The Chrome version on the store today (1.2.0) works only on chat.deepseek.com, gemini.google.com and notebook.google.com. Neither asks for access to any other site.
  • One thing is sent, and only if you buy Pro and activate a license key: the key and a random ID for this browser go to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider, to check the license (on activation also the extension version, and the browser and system name; in Firefox only if you allow technical data). Firefox asks for your permission first. Nothing about your chats is included.
  • Removing the extension deletes your library from the browser. Back it up first if you want to keep it.

Browsers

  • Firefox: available on Firefox Add-ons (version 2.0.0, approved 24 November 2026): Add to Firefox, free.
  • Chrome: available on the Chrome Web Store (version 1.2.0, published 21 November 2026): Add to Chrome, free. Version 2.0.0 is in Chrome Web Store review.

Which sites each version works on today:

  • Firefox 2.0.0: DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com), Gemini (gemini.google.com) and Gemini Notebook (notebook.google.com). ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Claude (claude.ai) on request: it asks for nothing at install, and works there after you click its toolbar button on that tab, or after you allow the site in its settings. You can turn each of the two off again.
  • Chrome 1.2.0: DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com), Gemini (gemini.google.com) and Gemini Notebook (notebook.google.com).

Not saved in 2.0: pictures (a picture stays as a short line, or as a link in Markdown), uploaded files, and the content of Claude artifacts (a title line is kept). In a notebook, only the chat is read, not your sources or notes. In Chrome 1.2.0, not saved: pictures (a picture inside a reply is kept as a link in Markdown only), uploaded files, Canvas documents and Deep Research reports. On Gemini and in Gemini Notebook, maths and the reasoning ("thinking") section are not supported in Chrome 1.2.0.
