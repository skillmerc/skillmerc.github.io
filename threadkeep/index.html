Threadkeep: save a DeepSeek chat as PDF, Markdown or Word

Status, 14 November 2026: Threadkeep 1.0.0 is submitted to Firefox Add-ons and waiting for Mozilla's review. Once it is approved it will be at addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/threadkeep-deepseek (that link works only after approval). The Chrome version is coming soon. It was tested on a real DeepSeek chat: 24 of 24 messages saved in order in every format, with code, tables, lists, formulas, Chinese and Arabic intact. Questions: kyle@skillmerc.com.

Threadkeep is a browser extension by SKILLMERC with one job: save the DeepSeek conversation you have open in your tab to a file on your own computer.

What it does

  1. Open a chat on chat.deepseek.com and click the Export button the extension adds to the page.
  2. Pick a format: Markdown, plain text, JSON, PDF, Word, or copy to the clipboard.
  3. The file is saved by your browser's normal download or print window.

It is built for the things that usually go wrong when a chat is copied by hand:

  • Long chats. A long DeepSeek chat only keeps the messages near the screen in the page. Threadkeep pages through the open chat once, from top to bottom, and joins the parts in order. If it could not join two parts it says so instead of leaving messages out without telling you.
  • Both sides. Your messages and the replies. The panel shows how many of each it read.
  • Real text in PDFs. PDF goes through your browser's own "Save as PDF", so the text can be selected and searched in any language, including Chinese, Arabic and Russian. No image pages and no watermark.
  • Code, tables and maths. Code blocks keep their language, tables become Markdown tables, formulas keep their LaTeX source, links stay links.
  • The reasoning section can be included or left out.

The Word export is a document file that Word opens (.doc made from HTML), not a .docx.

What stays on your device: everything

  • Your chat is read in your browser and saved to your computer. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We run no server that could receive it.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • It works only on chat.deepseek.com and asks for no access to any other site.
  • It reads the chat you have open, when you click. It does not use your DeepSeek login to fetch anything in the background.
  • One thing is sent, and only if you buy Pro and activate a license key: the key, a random install ID, the browser and operating system name and the extension version go to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider, to check the license. Nothing about your chats is included.
