Redeem a deal code for Threadkeep Pro

If you bought Threadkeep Pro on AppSumo, you received a one-time code. This page turns that code into a lifetime Pro license key. The total is USD 0.00 and you are not asked for a card.

The next screen is the checkout of Freemius, our licensing provider. It shows the Pro lifetime plan with your code applied (100% off) and a button "Upgrade for Free". If it shows a phone field, that field is optional: leave it empty.

Steps

  1. Install the extension. It works in Chrome and Firefox: Threadkeep in the Chrome Web Store, or Threadkeep on Firefox Add-ons.
  2. Fill in your name, email and deal code above and click "Redeem code".
  3. On the Freemius screen, check that the total is USD 0.00. Type your email again under "Confirm email address". If the screen shows two options about update emails, choose either one: the button does nothing until you do. Then click "Upgrade for Free". The next screen says "Purchase was successful" and does not show your key. Freemius emails the key to the address you entered. Keep this page open and come back for step 4.
  4. Open Threadkeep's Pro license page. Stay on this page: here Threadkeep's toolbar button opens that page. (On a chat site the same button opens the chat panel instead.) Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of the browser, then Threadkeep in its list. If you pinned Threadkeep, click its own icon in the toolbar instead. A new tab opens with Threadkeep's settings, called "Threadkeep: Pro license". You can also get there from an open chat: click "Pro and license" at the bottom of Threadkeep's panel. Do not click the "Get Pro" button on that page: it is for buying without a code. Paste your key into the field that says "Paste your license key (sk_...)" and click "Activate". Activating runs the license check. It sends your license key, a random install ID, the extension version and the browser and system name to Freemius (in Firefox, the browser and system name only if you allow technical data). Nothing about your chats is sent. If Firefox asks for permission to share authentication information with the developer, click "Allow": that is the license check, and Pro switches on only after you do. Chrome does not ask. The page then says "Pro is active on this browser."

Good to know

  • You do not need an account or password to use Pro. Your license key is your access, so keep the email.
  • One code gives one lifetime Pro license for one browser. To move it, click "Deactivate on this browser" on the Pro license page first, then activate the key in the other browser.
  • Free holds 100 saved chats, 5 folders (subfolders included) and 5 different tags, with 3 PDF or Word exports a day. Markdown, text, JSON and copy have no limit on Free, and search shows every result. Pro removes those limits and adds ZIP export of a folder or a search result, and "Continue in a new chat".
  • Each code works once. Codes stay valid for at least 60 days after you buy.
  • If the Freemius screen shows a total other than USD 0.00 or asks for a card, the code was not accepted (mistyped or already used). Do not pay. Go back, check the code, or email us.
  • If the "Redeem code" button does not take you to Freemius, open the Pro lifetime checkout yourself. Click "Have a promotional code?", paste your code and click Apply. The total drops to USD 0.00. Enter your name and email and click "Upgrade for Free". Your license key arrives by email.
  • Refunds are handled by AppSumo under its 60-day refund policy. When a code is refunded, its license is cancelled.
  • Stuck? Email kyle@skillmerc.com with your code and we will sort it out.
