List & Table Scraper: guides

Practical guides for getting tables and lists from websites into a spreadsheet. The add-on works in Firefox and Chrome.

  • How to export a Wikipedia table to Excel, Google Sheets or CSV (3 free ways)
    Three free ways to get a table from Wikipedia into a spreadsheet: copy and paste, Google Sheets IMPORTHTML, or a browser add-on that also keeps the article links. When to use each one.
  • How to scrape a list from multiple pages into Excel or CSV without code
    Search results, directories and product listings are split across pages. How to collect every page into one spreadsheet without writing code, what Excel and Google Sheets can and cannot do, and how to stay polite to the website.
  • How to scrape an infinite scroll page into Excel or CSV
    Pages that load more items as you scroll defeat copy and paste, IMPORTHTML and Power Query. Why that happens, the manual workarounds, and how to collect the list into a spreadsheet with a browser add-on for Firefox or Chrome.
  • IMPORTHTML not working in Google Sheets? Why it fails and how to get the table anyway
    IMPORTHTML returns "Imported content is empty", "Could not fetch url" or the wrong table. The usual causes (JavaScript tables, logins, blocked requests, wrong index) and a free way to get the data into Sheets anyway.
  • How to scrape product cards or search results (not a table) into Excel or Google Sheets
    Product grids, search results and directory cards are not HTML tables, so table tools miss them or squash them into one column. Free ways that need no install, what each one cannot do, and a browser add-on that turns each card into a row.
  • How to scrape a table that spans several pages into Excel or Google Sheets
    A table that shows 10 or 25 rows at a time with a Next button. How to check whether all rows are already in the page, how to stack pages with IMPORTHTML or Power Query when the page number is in the address, and what to do when it is not.
