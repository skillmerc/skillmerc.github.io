List & Table Scraper: guides

Practical guides for getting tables and lists from websites into a spreadsheet.

  • How to export a Wikipedia table to Excel, Google Sheets or CSV (3 free ways)
    Three free ways to get a table from Wikipedia into a spreadsheet: copy and paste, Google Sheets IMPORTHTML, or a browser add-on that also keeps the article links. When to use each one.
  • How to scrape a list from multiple pages into Excel or CSV without code
    Search results, directories and product listings are split across pages. How to collect every page into one spreadsheet without writing code, what Excel and Google Sheets can and cannot do, and how to stay polite to the website.
  • How to scrape an infinite scroll page into Excel or CSV
    Pages that load more items as you scroll defeat copy and paste, IMPORTHTML and Power Query. Why that happens, the manual workarounds, and how to collect the list into a spreadsheet with a Firefox add-on.
  • IMPORTHTML not working in Google Sheets? Why it fails and how to get the table anyway
    IMPORTHTML returns "Imported content is empty", "Could not fetch url" or the wrong table. The usual causes (JavaScript tables, logins, blocked requests, wrong index) and a free way to get the data into Sheets anyway.
