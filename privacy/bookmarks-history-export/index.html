Privacy: Export Bookmarks & History: CSV, Excel, JSON

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Export Bookmarks & History: CSV, Excel, JSON is a browser extension made by SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd, South Africa. This page says what it reads, what it does with it and what it sends. Questions: kyle@skillmerc.com

What it reads

  • Your bookmarks: folder, title, address and the date each was added.
  • Your browsing history: page title, address, visit count and the time of the last visit, for the dates you choose.
  • It reads them only after you click "I agree" on the notice it shows the first time you open it. Page titles and bookmark titles are text that came from websites, and the addresses are links to them: that is all the website content it handles. It cannot open or read the pages themselves, and it has no access to passwords, cookies or forms.
  • If you sync between devices, the history list the browser gives can include pages opened on your other devices. Titles and addresses can contain personal details, for example an email address in the title of a mail page. They are handled only as rows in the list and in the file you export.

What it does with it

It lists them on its own page in your browser and, when you click an export button, writes the rows shown to a file in your downloads folder (CSV, Excel, JSON or bookmarks HTML). That is the only use.

What it keeps

  • In the browser's local extension storage: that you agreed to the notice, and which list and date range you used last. Removing the extension deletes both.
  • It keeps no copy of your bookmarks or history. The files you export are ordinary files on your computer, under your control.

What it sends

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. SKILLMERC never receives your bookmarks, your history or anything else from it.

Other parties

None. No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Your choices

You can decline the notice, and nothing is read. You can remove the extension at any time from the browser's extensions page. The files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them; they contain your browsing history, so share them with care.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension uses the bookmarks and history it reads only to provide its single purpose, exporting them to a file for you, and does not transfer them, use them for advertising or let any person read them.

Changes

If a later version changes what is read, kept or sent, this page changes first and the extension asks again before it reads anything.
