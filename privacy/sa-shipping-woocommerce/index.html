Privacy Policy: Skillmerc Shipping for The Courier Guy, Pudo and PAXI

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers the free WooCommerce plugin "Skillmerc Shipping for The Courier Guy, Pudo and PAXI" and its optional paid add-on "Skillmerc Shipping Pro", both made by SKILLMERC (PTY) LTD, South Africa.

What SKILLMERC receives

The free plugin sends nothing to SKILLMERC. It has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and SKILLMERC runs no server that the plugin talks to. Your settings, your courier API keys and your orders stay in your own WordPress database.

What the plugin sends to the couriers

The plugin runs on your store and calls the couriers directly with your own courier account keys. It sends data only when rates are calculated in the cart or checkout, when the Pudo locker list is refreshed (at most every 12 hours), or when the customer opens the PAXI map.

  • The Courier Guy (ShipLogic) (api.shiplogic.com for sandbox keys, api.portal.thecourierguy.co.za for live keys): your sender address, the customer's delivery address (street, suburb, city, province, postal code, company), the parcel size and weight, and the cart value as declared value. Privacy policy: https://thecourierguy.co.za/privacy-policy/
  • Pudo (The Courier Guy Locker) (sandbox.api-pudo.co.za for sandbox, api-tcg.co.za for live): your sender address, the chosen locker code and the parcel size and weight. Privacy policy: https://thecourierguy.co.za/privacy-policy/
  • Fastway Couriers South Africa (sa.api.fastway.org): your API key, your pickup franchise code, the customer's town or suburb and postal code, and the parcel weight. Privacy: https://www.fastway.co.za/media/2467/privacy-and-protection-of-personal-information-fastway-june-2021.pdf
  • PAXI map (map.paxi.co.za): when the customer clicks "Choose a PAXI point", the map loads in a frame in the customer's browser from PAXI's servers, which see the customer's IP address and browser details, and it may use Google Maps and Google Analytics. The plugin itself sends nothing to PAXI. Only the chosen point (code, name, address) comes back to your store. Terms and privacy policy: https://www.paxi.co.za/terms-conditions

The chosen locker or PAXI point is saved on the order in your own store.

Pro add-on (optional)

When you book a shipment from the order screen, Pro sends the order's delivery details (recipient name, address, phone and email as held on the order), your sender details and the parcel size and weight to The Courier Guy through the same ShipLogic API, using your own key, so the courier can collect and deliver the parcel. The tracking reference and label come back and are stored on the order in your store.

Pro includes the Freemius SDK (freemius.com), our payment and licensing provider, for licence checks and updates. When you activate a licence it sends your licence key, your site address and the WordPress, PHP and plugin versions to Freemius. Freemius may also receive the site administrator's name and email and basic site diagnostics, which you can opt out of on the activation screen. Purchases are handled by Freemius as merchant of record under its own privacy policy: https://freemius.com/privacy/

Your role and ours

You, the store owner, decide what customer data your store collects and you are responsible for your own store's privacy notice. The couriers handle the delivery data under their own policies. SKILLMERC only sees personal information you choose to send us, for example in a support email, and we use it only to answer you.
