Threadkeep privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy is published at skillmerc.com/privacy/threadkeep. Publisher: SKILLMERC (PTY) LTD, South Africa. Contact: kyle@skillmerc.com.

This one policy covers Threadkeep in the Chrome Web Store and on Firefox Add-ons.

Older versions

This policy describes Threadkeep 2.0.2. Until 2.0.2 reaches your browser, you may still have an older version. It reads, stores and sends less than this page describes, never more:

  • Versions 2.0.0 and 2.0.1 do not add the "Saved with Threadkeep" line to files, and in Chrome they do not ask for a rating or keep the rating count.
  • Chrome version 1.2.0 works on DeepSeek, Gemini and Gemini Notebook only. It has no ChatGPT or Claude access and no saved chats (no library, no Save button, no "Delete all saved chats"), and it does not use the activeTab or scripting permission.
  • Firefox version 1.0.0 works on DeepSeek only. It does not show the notice described below: it reads the open chat when you open its panel and when you export. It has no saved chats, does not keep the rating count, and asks only for storage and access to chat.deepseek.com.
  • In Chrome 1.2.0 and Firefox 1.0.0, the licence check to Freemius sends the same data as described under "What leaves your device", and there is no extra re-check after a Free limit.

Short version

Threadkeep reads the AI chat you have open, in your browser, and turns it into a file or a saved copy on your own device. Your chats are never sent to us or to anyone else. We run no server that could receive them. The only thing Threadkeep sends over the internet is a licence check to Freemius, and only if you enter a Pro licence key.

Where it works

Threadkeep works on these sites only:

  • DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com)
  • Gemini (gemini.google.com)
  • Gemini Notebook (notebook.google.com): only the notebook's chat, not its sources or notes
  • ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Claude (claude.ai): only after you click the Threadkeep button on that tab, or after you allow the site in Threadkeep's settings. You can turn each of these sites off again on the options page.

It does not read any other site.

What it reads, and when

Before Threadkeep reads anything, it shows a short notice. Nothing is read until you click "I agree". After that, it reads the conversation on the page you have open (your messages and the replies) when you open its panel, when you export, and when you press Save. It does not read other chats in the site's sidebar, and it does not use your login on these sites to fetch anything in the background. On a long chat, after your click, it scrolls that one chat for you so the site shows the earlier messages, then puts the chat back where you were (on DeepSeek you can switch this off and scroll yourself). It never calls the chat site's servers itself. These are your own conversations: your use of DeepSeek, Gemini, Gemini Notebook, ChatGPT and Claude stays under each site's own terms.

What it stores, and where

Everything below stays in your browser on your device.

  • Saved chats (the library): when you press Save, a copy of the open chat goes into a database inside the extension, in your browser. For each chat it keeps the site, the chat's address and id, its title, when it was saved and updated, the message count, its folder, pins and tags, and the text of each message. It also keeps your folders and a search index built from the saved text. Pictures are not downloaded or saved; a picture can stay as a short line or a link to its web address.
  • Exported files: Markdown, text, JSON, Word and PDF files are saved by your browser's normal download or print window. For PDF, the chat is held in the browser for a moment so the print page can show it, then deleted.
  • Your agreement to the notice: one flag.
  • A daily count of PDF and Word exports, so the free limit can work.
  • Your licence state, if you enter a Pro key (see below).
  • A count of finished file exports (Markdown, text, JSON, Word or PDF; Copy does not count) and the state of the rating question (not asked yet, asked, or your answer: Rate it or No thanks), stored in your browser so the extension asks you once, after your third file export, whether you would like to rate it on the store you installed it from. If you click "Rate it", the store's reviews page opens in a new tab; nothing is sent by the extension. The question is never shown again.

What it adds to your files

On the Free plan, Markdown, text, Word and PDF exports end with one line, "Saved with Threadkeep", and a link to skillmerc.com/threadkeep/. The link has fixed tags (utm_source=threadkeep, utm_medium=export, utm_campaign=saved-with) that only tell us a visit came from an exported file. It holds nothing about you, your chats or your browser, and Threadkeep sends nothing when it adds the line. You can switch the line off in Threadkeep's settings. JSON files, copied text and Pro exports never have it. If you open the link, our website's privacy notice applies: skillmerc.com/privacy/website.

What leaves your device

Only the licence check, and only if you enter a Pro licence key. Threadkeep talks to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider:

  • When you activate a key, it sends your licence key, a random ID the extension made for this browser, the browser and operating system name (for example "Chrome on macOS"; in Firefox only if you allow technical data) and the extension version.
  • When it re-checks or deactivates a key, it sends your licence key, the same random ID and the install number Freemius gave at activation.

While a key is stored, Threadkeep re-checks it on its own at most once a day. It also re-checks right after you activate, when you press "Re-check license", and when a save hits a Free limit while your licence shows as active (at most once a minute). Like any web request, Freemius's servers see the IP address the check comes from and your browser's usual request headers. Nothing about your chats is included. In Firefox, the browser asks your permission for this data before the first check, and no check is sent without it. Purchases are handled by Freemius under its own privacy policy (freemius.com/privacy).

Nothing else is sent. Backups and exports are files you download yourself.

What we do not do

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data, no remote code. The Free plan's 'Saved with Threadkeep' line, described above, is a link to our own product page; it is not an ad from anyone else and the extension sends nothing when it adds it.

Limited use

Our use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • storage: your agreement, the daily export count, the licence state, the short-lived print job, the rating count and your choice for the "Saved with Threadkeep" line.
  • Access to chat.deepseek.com, gemini.google.com and notebook.google.com: to show the Threadkeep button and read the open chat there.
  • Optional access to chatgpt.com and claude.ai: asked only when you choose to show Threadkeep on that site every time.
  • activeTab: lets one click on the Threadkeep button work on the tab you are on, once, without a standing permission.
  • scripting: to start Threadkeep on ChatGPT or Claude after you allow that site, and to stop it when you turn the site off.

Deleting your data

  • One saved chat: delete it in the library. Deleting a folder moves its chats to "Unfiled"; it does not delete them.
  • Everything you saved: "Delete all saved chats" on the options page.
  • Withdrawing your agreement on the options page stops all reading. It does not delete your saved chats; delete them with the button next to it.
  • Deactivate a licence on the options page.
  • Removing the extension: the browser then deletes what Threadkeep stored in it, saved chats included. To keep your saved chats, download a backup from the library first.

Children

Threadkeep is not made for children and we collect no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
