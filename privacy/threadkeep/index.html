Threadkeep privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. This policy is published at https://skillmerc.com/privacy/threadkeep/. Publisher: SKILLMERC (PTY) LTD, South Africa. Contact: kyle@skillmerc.com.

Short version

Threadkeep reads the DeepSeek conversation you have open, in your browser, when you click an export button, and saves it to your own computer. Your conversations are never sent to us or to anyone else. We run no server that could receive them.

What the extension handles

  • Conversation content (your messages and the replies on the chat page you have open): read in the page when you click an export button, turned into a file, and saved by your browser's normal download or print window. For PDF it is held in the browser's memory for a moment so the print page can show it, then deleted. It is not stored by the extension after that and is not transmitted.
  • A daily counter of PDF and Word exports: stored in your browser so the free limit can work. Not transmitted.

What is sent, and only if you buy Pro

When you paste a license key and click Activate, the extension sends your license key, a random install ID made by the extension, the browser and operating system name, and the extension version to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider, to check the license. It repeats that check at most once a day. Nothing about your conversations is included. In Firefox the browser asks your permission for this first. Purchases are handled by Freemius under its own privacy policy (https://freemius.com/privacy/).

What we do not do

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data, no remote code, no reading of any site other than chat.deepseek.com, no use of your DeepSeek login to fetch anything in the background.

Permissions

  • storage: the daily counter, the license state, and the short-lived print job.
  • Access to https://chat.deepseek.com/*: to show the Export button and read the open conversation there.

Your choices

Remove the extension to delete everything it stored. Deactivate a license on the options page. Questions or requests: kyle@skillmerc.com.

Threadkeep is an independent tool and is not affiliated with DeepSeek.
