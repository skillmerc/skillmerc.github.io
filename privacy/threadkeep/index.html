Threadkeep privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. This policy is published at skillmerc.com/privacy/threadkeep. Publisher: SKILLMERC (PTY) LTD, South Africa. Contact: kyle@skillmerc.com.

Short version

Threadkeep reads the AI chat conversation you have open on DeepSeek (chat.deepseek.com), Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Gemini Notebook (notebook.google.com), in your browser, when you click an export button, and saves it to your own computer. In Gemini Notebook only the notebook's chat is read, not its sources or notes. Your conversations are never sent to us or to anyone else. We run no server that could receive them. The Firefox add-on works on DeepSeek only.

What the extension handles

  • Conversation content (your messages and the replies on the chat page you have open): read in the page when you click an export button, turned into a file, and saved by your browser's normal download or print window. For PDF it is held in the browser's memory for a moment so the print page can show it, then deleted. It is not stored by the extension after that and is not transmitted.
  • A daily counter of PDF and Word exports: stored in your browser so the free limit can work. Not transmitted.
  • Your one-time agreement to the notice the Chrome extension shows before it first reads a chat: one flag stored in your browser. Not transmitted.
  • Pictures: the extension does not download or save pictures. In a Markdown file a picture inside a reply is kept as a link to its web address.
  • In builds that show a rating question (the Firefox add-on from version 1.0.1): a count of finished exports and one yes/no flag, stored in your browser so the add-on asks you once, after your third export, whether you would like to rate it. If you click "Rate Threadkeep", the store's reviews page opens in a new tab; nothing is sent by the add-on. The question is never shown again. The Chrome extension does not show this question and stores no such count.

What is sent, and only if you buy Pro

When you activate, re-check or deactivate a license key, the extension sends your license key, a random install ID made by the extension, the browser and operating system name, and the extension version to Freemius, our payment and licensing provider, to check the license. While a key is stored, it repeats that check at most once a day. As with any web request, Freemius's servers see the IP address the check comes from. Nothing about your conversations is included. In Firefox the browser asks your permission for this first. Purchases are handled by Freemius under its own privacy policy (freemius.com/privacy).

What we do not do

No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data, no remote code, no reading of any site other than chat.deepseek.com, gemini.google.com and notebook.google.com, no use of your DeepSeek or Google login to fetch anything in the background.

Limited use

Our use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • storage: the daily counter, the license state, your agreement to the first-use notice, the short-lived print job and, in the Firefox add-on, the export count for the one-time rating question.
  • Access to chat.deepseek.com* and, in the Chrome extension, gemini.google.com* and notebook.google.com*: to show the Export button and read the open conversation there.

Your choices

Remove the extension to delete everything it stored. Withdraw your agreement to the first-use notice on the Chrome extension's options page. Deactivate a license on the options page. Questions or requests: kyle@skillmerc.com.
