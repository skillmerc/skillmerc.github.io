Privacy: Changekeep: Page Change Monitor and Alerts

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Changekeep is a browser add-on made by SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd, South Africa. This page says what it reads, what it keeps, what it sends and to whom. Questions: kyle@skillmerc.com

In short: Changekeep contacts only the sites you choose to watch and, for Pro, our licence provider Freemius. Nothing is sent to SKILLMERC: no account, no analytics.

What it reads

  • The part of a web page you pick: its text, and the address and title of that page. It reads it on the tab where you press the Changekeep button and choose the part. Before your first pick it shows a notice, and it reads nothing until you click "I agree".
  • To check a watch, it loads that page again and reads the same part (see "What it sends").
  • It does not read your browsing history, bookmarks, passwords, forms or cookies. A page you watch can show personal details, for example your name on an order page. They are handled only as the watched text, in your browser.

What it does with it

It compares the part you picked with what it read last time. When it changed, it tells you: a browser notification, a count on the toolbar button, and a change view with the text before and after. That is the only use.

What it keeps, and where

Everything is kept in your browser's local storage for the add-on, on this computer:

  • your watches: the page address and title, which part you picked, and your settings for that watch;
  • the last text read for each watch, cut to 20,000 characters;
  • up to 50 past changes for each watch, each side cut to 4,000 characters;
  • your settings, and that you agreed to the notice;
  • for Pro only, once Pro is on sale: your licence key and the licence details Freemius sends back (an install ID, the plan and the expiry date). Version 1.0 is free and keeps none of this.

Nothing is kept anywhere else. SKILLMERC has no server that holds your watches. If you export your watch list, the file is an ordinary file on your computer, under your control.

What it sends

To the sites you watch. To check a watch, the add-on loads that page from your browser, on the schedule you set, at most once a minute per site. The site sees an ordinary visit from your browser, as when you open the page yourself, including your IP address and your browser's usual details. The request goes without your sign-in. If you tick "This page needs me signed in" for a watch, your browser adds that site's own cookies to the request for that site only. The add-on itself never reads those cookies. After you save a watch for a new site, the watch list asks you to allow Changekeep to open that site.

To Freemius, for Pro only, once Pro is on sale. Pro is not on sale yet. Version 1.0 is free and contacts no licence server. When Pro is on sale, if you buy it and activate a licence key, the add-on will send Freemius, our payment and licence provider, your licence key, a random install ID made by the add-on, your browser and operating system name (for example "Firefox on Windows") and the add-on's version. It will do this when you activate, about once a day to check that the licence is still valid, and when you deactivate. Before the first activation, Firefox will ask you to allow this. Nothing about your watches or the pages you watch will be included. The free version contacts no one but the sites you watch. Purchases will be handled by Freemius under its own privacy policy: freemius.com/privacy.

To SKILLMERC: nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Your choices

  • Decline the notice, and nothing is read.
  • Pause or delete any watch. Deleting a watch deletes its text and its changes. When you delete the last watch for a site, the add-on gives back its permission to open that site.
  • Take back a site's permission at any time in Firefox's add-on settings.
  • "Delete everything" in Changekeep's settings deletes every watch, every change and your settings.
  • For Pro, once it is on sale: deactivate the licence on this browser from the add-on's licence page.
  • Removing the add-on deletes everything it kept, including the licence details.

Changes to this page

If a later version changes what is read, kept or sent, this page changes first and the add-on asks again before it reads anything.
