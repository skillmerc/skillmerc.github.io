Privacy Policy: List & Table Scraper

Last updated: 24 November 2026

List & Table Scraper reads the content of a web page only when you click its toolbar button, so it can show you the rows and export them. That content is handled inside your browser. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it leaves the page only when you download or copy it. The add-on keeps no history of the pages you visit.

  • There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • All detection, preview and export happens inside your browser. The add-on has no server of its own.
  • Data you scrape stays on your device. It only leaves the page when you choose to download a CSV, Excel or JSON file or copy it to your clipboard.
  • The add-on can read a page only after you click its toolbar button on that tab (the "activeTab" permission). It does not run on other tabs.
  • When you use "Crawl pages" and the site's Next control is a normal link, the add-on loads those next pages from the same website you are on, the same way your browser would if you clicked them.

Pro license (optional)

The free version reads up to 3 pages per run. If you buy Pro and click Activate on the add-on's Pro license page, the add-on sends these to Freemius (freemius.com), our payment and licensing provider, to check the license:

  • your license key,
  • a random install ID created by the add-on,
  • the browser and operating system name (for example "Firefox on macOS"),
  • the add-on version.

This happens when you activate, re-check or deactivate a key, and at most once a day while Pro is in use. Nothing about the pages you visit or scrape is sent. The license details (your key, the install ID, the plan and its expiry) and any recipes you save are stored locally in your browser (storage permission). A recipe holds the website's hostname, which block and Next button to use there, the wait and the page limit. Recipes are never sent anywhere. The license details go only to Freemius, as described above. As with any web request, Freemius's servers see the IP address the license check comes from. In Firefox the add-on asks for this optional data permission when you click Activate, and you can revoke it in about:addons. Purchases are handled by Freemius as merchant of record under its own privacy policy.

Purchases and email. If you buy Pro or redeem an AppSumo code, Freemius shares your name and email address with us. We use them to deliver and support your license. We may send you one email that asks how the add-on is working and links to the review page. We never add you to a mailing list and never sell your details. To ask us to delete them, write to kyle@skillmerc.com.

Our use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
