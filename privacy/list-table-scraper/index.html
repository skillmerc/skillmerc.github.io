Privacy Policy: List & Table Scraper

Last updated: 10 November 2026

List & Table Scraper does not collect, store or send any browsing data. Data you scrape never leaves your browser unless you download or copy it.

  • There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • All detection, preview and export happens inside your browser. The add-on has no server of its own.
  • Data you scrape stays on your device. It only leaves the page when you choose to download a CSV, Excel or JSON file or copy it to your clipboard.
  • The add-on can read a page only after you click its toolbar button on that tab (the "activeTab" permission). It does not run on other tabs.
  • When you use "Crawl pages" and the site's Next control is a normal link, the add-on loads those next pages from the same website you are on, the same way your browser would if you clicked them.

Pro license (optional)

The free version reads up to 3 pages per run. If you buy Pro and click Activate on the add-on's Pro license page, the add-on sends these to Freemius (freemius.com), our payment and licensing provider, to check the license:

  • your license key,
  • a random install ID created by the add-on,
  • the browser and operating system name (for example "Firefox on macOS"),
  • the add-on version.

This happens when you activate, re-check or deactivate a key, and at most once a day while Pro is in use. Nothing about the pages you visit or scrape is sent. The license state and any recipes you save (which block and Next button to use on a website) are stored locally in your browser (storage permission) and are never sent anywhere. In Firefox the add-on asks for this optional data permission when you click Activate, and you can revoke it in about:addons. Purchases are handled by Freemius as merchant of record under its own privacy policy.
