Privacy Policy: List & Table Scraper

Last updated: 11 October 2026

List & Table Scraper does not collect, store or send any personal data or browsing data.

  • There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • The add-on has no server of its own. All detection, preview and export happens inside your browser.
  • Data you scrape stays on your device. It only leaves the page when you choose to download a CSV or Excel file or copy it to your clipboard.
  • The add-on can read a page only after you click its toolbar button on that tab (the "activeTab" permission). It does not run on other tabs or in the background.
  • When you use "Crawl pages" and the site's Next control is a normal link, the add-on loads those next pages from the same website you are on, the same way your browser would if you clicked them. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
