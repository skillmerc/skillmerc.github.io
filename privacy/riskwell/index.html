Riskwell for Jira: privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. Riskwell: Risk Register & Risk Matrix for Jira ("the app") is made by SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd, South Africa. Contact: kyle@skillmerc.com.

What the app does

The app keeps a risk register per Jira project: risks with likelihood and impact ratings, mitigation, owner, review dates and linked work items, plus the project's risk matrix. It sends review reminders through Jira's own notifications, and a Rovo agent can read the register.

Where data is kept

The app is built on Atlassian Forge. Its code runs on Atlassian's infrastructure and everything it stores is kept in Forge storage inside your Atlassian site's app installation. SKILLMERC does not run servers for this app and does not receive a copy of your data. The app makes no requests to any service outside Atlassian.

What the app stores

  • Per project: the risk matrix (level names, band thresholds, review interval).
  • Per risk: the title, description, category, ratings, mitigation, treatment, status, review dates, the keys of linked work items, the owner's Atlassian account id, the account ids of the people who created, last edited and last reviewed it, and the review date a reminder was last sent for.
  • A list of the projects that have a register, so the daily reminder job knows where to look.

The app stores Atlassian account ids only. It does not store names, email addresses or avatars. Owner names are looked up from Jira when the CSV export is made and are not stored. Do not put passwords, tokens or other secrets into risk text.

What the app reads from Jira

The project, the summary, description and status of work items you link or ask the agent about, and your own permissions in the project. Reads for people on screen run as that person, so they only see what Jira lets them see.

Reminders

The daily reminder uses Jira's notify feature on a linked work item. The email is sent by Jira to the risk owner and contains the risk id, title and review date.

Rovo

If you use the Riskwell agent, its answers are produced by Atlassian Rovo from the data described above. See Atlassian's own privacy terms for Rovo.

Logs

The app writes technical logs to Forge logging: record ids, counts and error types. It does not log risk text or names.

Deleting data

Deleting a risk in the register removes it from storage. Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage.
