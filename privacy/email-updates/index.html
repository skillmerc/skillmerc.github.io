Privacy: email updates

You can ask for an email when SKILLMERC ships something new or runs a deal for one of its tools. This page says what we keep, why, and how to stop it.

Who we are

SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in South Africa (registration number 2025/673155/07). We are the responsible party under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the controller under the EU and UK GDPR. Contact: kyle@skillmerc.com.

How signing up works

  1. You type your email address into a signup form on skillmerc.com, tick the box to say you agree, and press the button.
  2. We send one email with a link to confirm. Nothing is added to the list until you press it.
  3. If you do not press it, we send nothing more, and we delete the request after 30 days.

What we keep

  • Your email address and which tool you asked about.
  • On the Switch to FreeAgent waitlist only: the system you are moving from, if you choose one. It is optional. We use it only to count which systems people are moving from.
  • A record of your consent: the version of the wording the form showed, the time you signed up, the page you signed up on, and the time you confirmed.
  • A one-way code made from your IP address with a secret key. We use it only to stop abuse, such as one computer signing up many addresses. We never store the IP address itself, and we clear the code after 30 days.
  • When we sent you mail, so we can stay inside our own limits.

We keep your address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

We do not use tracking pixels or tracked links in these emails. We do not buy, sell, rent or share the list.

Why

To email you about new releases and deals for the tool you chose, because you asked us to (your consent). We email only when we have something new: a few times a year.

How to unsubscribe

Every email has an unsubscribe link. Press it, then press the Unsubscribe button. You do not need to sign in. You can also email kyle@skillmerc.com with the word Unsubscribe and we will remove you by hand within one working day.

When you unsubscribe we delete your email address from the list. We keep only a one-way code of it, which tool it was for, the page, the system you chose (if any) and the dates, so a mistake on our side cannot add it back. If you sign up again later, you confirm again.

Where it is kept

On our own database (Supabase, servers in the United States) behind our own service (Railway). Confirmation emails are sent through Google Workspace from kyle@skillmerc.com. These providers process the data for us under their terms. Data stored in the United States leaves South Africa and the EU; we rely on your consent and on those providers' standard data protection terms for that.

Your rights

Under POPIA and the GDPR you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or give it to you in a common format. You can withdraw consent at any time; that does not affect mail we sent before. Email kyle@skillmerc.com and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za), or, in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change how we use your email address, we will ask you again before we use it the new way. This page was written on 21 November 2026.
