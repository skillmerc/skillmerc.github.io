SKILLMERC affiliate terms

These terms cover the affiliate programmes for Threadkeep and List & Table, run by SKILLMERC (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in South Africa (2025/673155/07). Applying means you accept them.

What you earn

  • 30 percent of each payment from a new customer who buys through your link or your coupon, worked on what the customer paid after discounts and coupons, without sales tax or VAT.
  • On monthly and yearly plans, you earn on their payments for the first 12 months. On a lifetime licence, you earn once.
  • Your link is tracked for 60 days after a click. If someone else's link is clicked first, they get the credit.
  • No commission on our own customers who already paid, on free installs, or on purchases made with your own account or card.

When and how you are paid

  • A commission is confirmed once the sale is 60 days old and has not been refunded or disputed.
  • We pay by PayPal once a month, after the 10th, when your confirmed balance is USD 100 or more. Lower balances roll over.
  • We pay commission only on payments we have received. If a sale is refunded or charged back after we paid you, we take that commission off your next payouts.
  • You handle any tax due on what you earn.

How you may promote us

  • Tell people you earn a commission, every time, close to the link. For example: "I get a commission if you buy through this link." In a video, say it out loud as well as in the description. The words "affiliate link" alone are not enough.
  • Say what the tool does, honestly. No income claims, no "free forever" claims, no fake urgency, no claims we have not made.
  • No spam: no unsolicited email or direct messages, no comment spam, no posting in groups or forums that ban promotion.
  • Do not send your link by email, newsletters included, and do not put it in a mobile app. To promote us by email, ask us for an affiliate coupon and use that instead.
  • No paid search ads on our names (Threadkeep, List & Table, SKILLMERC) or misspellings of them, and no domains or social accounts that use those names.
  • No fake reviews, review swaps or ratings in exchange for anything, on any store or site.
  • No coupon or deal sites, and no deal groups or lifetime-deal communities (for example StackCommerce, Dealify, DealFuel or Lifetime Tech Deals). Do not post your coupon publicly unless we agree it in writing. Promote only the coupon we gave you, not other SKILLMERC codes, and never offer cashback or share your commission with buyers.
  • Comparisons and round-ups that also list other tools are fine.
  • Your site's privacy policy must say that you use a referral cookie (the Freemius Referral Policy gives the wording).
  • Do not suggest you are SKILLMERC, or that the tool is made or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek or any site it works with.
  • No cookie stuffing, pop-ups, hidden links or browser add-ons that insert your link.

How to join

Apply by email to kyle@skillmerc.com with a link to your site or channel. You do not need to be a customer to apply this way.

Already a customer? You can also apply in the customer portal: customers.freemius.com/store/20729 (Earn > Become an Affiliate).

Our side

  • We approve each application by hand and may say no without a reason.
  • We may change these terms or end the programme with 30 days' notice by email. Commission already confirmed is still paid.
  • If you break these terms, we may remove you and keep any unpaid commission from the sales involved.

Questions: kyle@skillmerc.com.
